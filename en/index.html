01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

How can a world model become a production tool?

World models can suggest a convincing world. Production needs measurable spatial evidence, persistent scene structure, editable assets, deterministic execution and validation.

My work explores the layer between those two: turning uncertain understanding into decisions a 3D pipeline can inspect and execute.

05 / FAILURE → DIAGNOSIS → SYSTEM DESIGN

Where world models break.

The useful result is not only a better image.
It is knowing what the next system must control.

01 / SPATIAL UNCERTAINTY

Single-view depth ambiguity.

A plausible view leaves scale, occlusion and camera pose unresolved. Additional views do not help if they cannot be reliably registered.

Decision / Keep unsupported space explicit.

02 / REPRESENTATION GAP

Reconstruction is not production geometry.

WorldSculpt's early ~529k-vertex / ~939k-face output was fragmented. A repaired surface still carried cost and topology limitations.

Decision / Inspect geometry, not just the preview.

03 / PLACEMENT AUTHORITY

An asset is not a scene decision.

A generated chair can look right and still disagree with source-space depth and silhouette. Appearance does not establish placement.

Decision / Fit and validate before acceptance.

07 / CAPABILITY BOUNDARIES

Different systems.
Different authority.

A pipeline becomes reliable when each component is responsible for what it can actually establish.

SYSTEMCAN PROVIDECANNOT DECIDE

01World Model

CAN PROVIDE

Semantics, object relationships and rough spatial hypotheses.

CANNOT DECIDE

Exact XYZ, metric scale or production geometry.

02Marble

CAN PROVIDE

Reconstruction evidence, camera cues and source-space observations.

CANNOT DECIDE

A trustworthy, production-ready mesh.

03Asset Generator

CAN PROVIDE

Candidate object geometry and appearance.

CANNOT DECIDE

Correct placement in the world.

04Scene Graph

CAN PROVIDE

Accepted decisions, provenance, confidence and state.

CANNOT DECIDE

Missing evidence. Recording a decision does not prove it true.

05Compiler

CAN PROVIDE

Deterministic execution of accepted scene decisions.

CANNOT DECIDE

Unknown spatial facts or absent constraints.

Responsibility model—not a claim that every stage of WORLDIR v2 is complete.
